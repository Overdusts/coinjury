## Memecoins are extremely high risk
Memecoins are among the most speculative assets that exist. The overwhelming
majority lose most or all of their value, often suddenly and permanently. You can
lose everything you put in. Never spend money you cannot afford to lose entirely.

## The risk score is a heuristic and can be wrong
coinjury's score is an automated estimate built from public data. It can produce
**false negatives** (miss a real scam) and **false positives** (flag a legitimate
token). A low score is *not* a safety guarantee, and a high score is *not* proof of
fraud. On-chain and market data may be inaccurate, incomplete, cached, or delayed.
Always do your own research.

## The paper experiment is simulated
The "$20 experiment" uses fake money and simulated fills. It is a transparency
demonstration of how the tool's own calls age over time — not a track record you
should trade on, and not a recommendation.
